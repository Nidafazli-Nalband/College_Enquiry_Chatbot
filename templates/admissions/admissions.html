Admissions

Admissions at MMEC are based on entrance exam scores from KCET, COMEDK, and JEE Main. We offer undergraduate programs in various engineering disciplines.

Eligibility Criteria

Passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics
Minimum aggregate as per university guidelines
Valid score in KCET / COMEDK / JEE Main

Admission Process

Appear for entrance examination (KCET / COMEDK / JEE Main)
Participate in counseling
Submit documents & fees
Confirm admission

Important Dates

Admissions open during the official counseling window. Refer to KCET, COMEDK, and JEE Main websites for updated schedules.
